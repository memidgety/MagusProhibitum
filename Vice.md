The characters of Magus Prohibitum are working professionals. They contend with jobs that others would not dare do, and must swim up stream to leave a mark in life. This comes with a lot of stress which inevitably brings out the worst in a person - their [[5. Faults|Faults]]. Therefore each professional needs to clear their mind and steady their nerves by...

# Indulging in Vice
When a character indulges in their vice, they hone their mind and body, clearing some of their faults. A character should have a Vice and Vice Purveyor upon character creation. When they partake in a vice, they may visit their purveyor and enjoy, though this takes time and can only be done in [[13. Long Rests]]. 

To determine how many faults you clear, roll your lowest attribute. The number on the dice is how many faults you clear.

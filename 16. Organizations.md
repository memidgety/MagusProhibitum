The Organization, or Org for short, is what all the characters are working for. Whether made by one of the characters, or a legacy passed down to them, the Org is there for something greater. Together you all buy into the Org and make it grow and expand, making names for yourselves.

The Org is the driving motive of the party. If someone leaves, retires, or passes, another character can join the Organization to fill their place. This also leaves room for multiple characters per player, or more players that rotate out as the story unfolds.

Everyone in an organization is assumed to be honing their skills, whether they are on an active job, or not. Orgs, and on a greater whole, Magus Prohibitum, allows other Player's characters to gain experience as another crew does a job. This facilitates players who might not have played, to be able to keep up with the team.

In this chapter, different organization types will be discussed.


# Creating an Organization

Your Org is what guides the party, it is what you will focus on while playing. Organizations grant their employees special benefits that might not be found from just a lone ranger. Your organization type is not a restriction to what you do, it is a reminder of what your characters care about at the end of the day. Simply put, a crew of Racketeers might have to end a bloodline (Hitmen), move some contraband (Vice Lords), or help a political figure solve a murder(Civic Watch).
## Types

### Academic
An Organization dedicated to the pursuit of knowledge. From mechanics to magic - this organization maps the bleeding edge of mankind's knowledge. 
### Believers
Whether a political stance or a faceless God, believers are those who follow the words of someone greater than them. 
### Civic Watch
Keepers of the peace, private investigators, vigilantes. Civic watch organizations watch out for never-do-wells and react accordingly. 
### Racketeers
This org thrives on ill gotten gains. Racketeers generate financial profit through unlawful means such as fraud, extortion, bribery, or violence (not death per hitmen).
### Vice Lords
 Providers of entertainment; of all types. Vice Lords procure and sell goods, whether legal or illicit, to the highest bidding payer.
### Hitmen
Cut-throats for hire. Whether it be espionage or a contract killing, hitmen will do the dirty work that others wont. 


An Organization is how the party is seen by others. The police will generally react differently to hitmen, then they will to those apart of a civic watch. The Organization is also important so that the Gamemaster can focus on certain themes, rather than preparing for all eventualities. A reminder that the Organization is not a fixed state. Academics, though often seen in high regard by the denizens of the City of Crossroads, can shift to more cult like behaviors, studying how magic binds life together, so that they may unbind it. If the players wish to restructure their organization, a job can be made out of that restructuring so they may keep some benefits.

The Organization starts with 2 cash, tier 0 and 0 reputation.

# General Upgrades

**Employee Training** 
The Organization invests back in its workers. Characters not actively playing get +2exp when a job is completed.

**Garage**
Whether it be for a horse drawn carriage, boat, or a new automobile, the crew has a place to store their vehicle out of harms way, and out of detection.

**Workshop**
A fully kitted shop that allows for long term creations in the organization. A character need not put themselves in danger while working on their project.

**Communications**
New technology is enabling mankind to have more readily accessible communication devices. The org can openly broadcast their services, take calls, or discretely listen in to other calls

**Laboratory**
A lab with all the necessary tools to do long term research. The lab comes with **[[2. Core System#GRADE|High-Grade]]** items for research and development.

**Storefront**
The organization operates from the back of another business, whether it be owned or not. The business can store up to 1 **Fame** or **Infamy** for the organization. This can only be expended by doing a job to remove it.

**Compounding Interest** 
When finishing a job, gain +1 cash. Gain an additional cash for every 10 cash in the Orgs Safe (before putting cash into the safe).

Licensed and Certified
There is never a question about your credibility. This can only be used for legal purposes (you cannot take this upgrade to lie about the certification).

Hard workers
You can make yourself look like a common laborer and gain increased **impact** or **+1d** when hiding in crowds.

Artist's tools
The organization has complete access to tools and instruments of the arts -  masonry, a block of ice and sculpting tools, a drum kit. These items do not take a gear slot and can be worked into the job where applicable.

Smash and grab
When breaking is involved in a job, you can start in a controlled situation.

Cleaners
After finishing a target, the next roll to get away is a controlled roll.

False Papers
You get **+1d** when using false documents to fool someone.

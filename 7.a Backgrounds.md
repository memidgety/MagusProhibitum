The Characters background represents what they did before their current station. It doesn't mean what they are doing now - they may even reject what they did in the past - but it represents some knowledge that they have gained, and tells others what you have done in the past. Think of a background as a prompt for your current characters backstory.

Some examples of Backgrounds are:
*A coal worker*
*A writer*
*A Soldier*
*Running from the law*

# Invoking backgrounds
Though your character has current skills in which they keep sharp, there comes times when old knowledge comes to bear. When this happens, it is called "**Invoking backgrounds**".

You can invoke a background in two ways. 
1. If this action aligns with your background you get +1d
2. If this opposes your background you can decide if you want to take -1d, but if you succeed you get +1 exp (or clear 1 (2?) fault).

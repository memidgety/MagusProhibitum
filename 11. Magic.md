The most powerful form of creation and destruction known to man. Magic has been banned from private and civilian use since The Magus War of 1861. Magus Prohibitum fell shortly after a warlock ritualistically sacrificed tens of thousands of unknowing victims, showing the world how dangerous the magical arts could be. Compromises were made on both sides to ban the use of unsanctioned magic, as it was seen as too dangerous to be used unchecked, but too useful to be discarded entirely.

The United States of Arcana started to shift away from personal magic to more industrial ways to improve the lives of their citizens. Yet the ban did not extinguish magic. Those who still cling to the old ways continue to wield it in secret, defying the laws that sought to outlaw it. Magic can be learned by anyone gifted enough to sense the Flow. The Flow permeates through all life, shaping it to the world we know today. It can be manipulated if a person has enough concentration and willpower - bending reality to their will.

Though most use magic illegally, several people have earned sanction to use their magic. Sanctioned Magic is a serious and great privilege and only the most prestigious, talented, *or rich*, get the license. These wizards are put on a registry and monitored frequently by the Wardens of the State.

# Redirecting the Flow
When a character wishes to force the Flow to their will, they open their mind and senses to the current of Magic. The Flow is a hostile environment to the mind and altering it can lead to dizziness or death of the mind. Even when a spell is successful it can often leave the caster physically exhausted. 

The Flow is ambiguous by design. It should allow for anything to happen, but feel dangerous and strange - you can redirect it briefly, guide it, even contain it, but at the end a river cannot be tamed. When a person taps into the Flow, they change reality around them.

A character may use any mental [[3. Attributes|attribute]] to conjure up a spell - forcing the Flow to their whim. This is inherently difficult and always demands a cut - the only exception is that Arcana allows 1 cut spells to be made without cut (2 cut spells will still take 2 cut). Spells can take any form, but the more they effect reality, the more effort is required.

# Mana
The flow of magic is an ethereal force. But some individuals can harness this flow and store it within themselves. This is often called Mana among practitioners. Storing magic within one self is a powerful and dangerous act. This bottled magical force spends more time with the users consciousness, becoming more capable of being shaped by the wizard. Casting a spell with a store of mana does not incite the normal cut that comes along with using magic. However, if a wizard has any amount of stored mana, they take a -1d for any other action they roll - due to the amount of concentration and strain on the mind and body that the mana takes. 

Mana is difficult to obtain. Often a long stretch of time - from dozens of minutes to hours -  is needed to stockpile enough magical energies to become Mana. Wizards will often store mana in their bodies in preparation for a confrontation, though it is rumored that some wizards have learned to bottle mana into more portable forms.

## Enforcer
#### Starting Skills
- +2 **Skirmish** or **Break**
- +1 **Command** or **Will**
#### Traits
**Bodyguard**  4-Track
When you protect a teammate, you may make a skill check with 1 cut to reduce the wounds by that amount, you then take the reduced damage. When you gather info to anticipate possible threats in the current situation, you get +1 impact.

**Leader** 3-Track
When you set up an ally, you get **improved position** for the action.

**Mule** 3-Track
Add +2 to your load limits; for instance Light: 5; Normal: 7; Heavy: 8;  

**Hercules** 2-Track
When you push yourself, you *display superhuman levels of strength,* and you do not take cut from Toughness based actions.

**Just a Scratch** 3-Track
When you take the Heal Wounds [[13. Long Rests#Healing Wounds|long rest]] action, you heal half of your traits rounded up.

**Warded** 1-Track
Magic affecting you takes -1 impact. When dispelling magics, you do not take cut. 

**Caution to the Wind** 4-Track Trait
Mark to increase impact when attacking at close quarters.

## Hunter
#### Starting Skills
- +2 **Hunt** or **Skirmish**
- +1 **Finesse** or **Stalk**
#### Traits
**Focused Shot** □□□□ 4-Track Trait
Mark to **increase impact** when attacking at long range.

**Precise Aim** □□□ 3-Track Trait
You can target attacks precisely (such as when aiming for a weak point) **without cutting** for difficulty.

**High Ground** □□□□ 4-Track 
**Increase impact** on any successful action taken whilst you have the high ground.

**Urban Jungle** □□□□ 4-Track
Mark to sprint up a surface that would usually require you to climb it, even if it’s unstable.

**Magical Dowsing** □□□ 3-Track Trait
You gain **+1 impact** when tracking supernatural sources (including supernatural races).

**Singular Mark** □□ 2-Track Trait
You may choose a **target** to be your focus. Any roll made to finishing your objective against said target gain **+1d** or **+1 impact**

**Mans Best Friend** □□□ 3-Track Trait
You gain a hunting companion. It obeys you to the best of its ability.

## Sleuth
#### Starting Skills
- +2 **Intuit** or **Study**
- +1 **Stalk** or **Finesse**
#### Traits
**Discerning Eye** 4-Track
You can tell a fake, forgery, or counterfeit item at a glance.

**Truthseeker** 3-Track
You can always tell when someone is lying to you.

**Cloak & Dagger** 3-Track
When you use a disguise or other form of covert misdirection, you get **+1d** to rolls to confuse or deflect suspicion. When you throw off your disguise, the resulting surprise gives you the initiative in the situation.

**Mesmer** 3-Track
When you socially interact with someone, you may cause them to forget that it’s happened until they next interact with you.

**Silver Tongue** 3-Track Trait
Gain +1d when making an excuse to not have a confrontation (physical or social). You must leave the area after this.

**Plant and Palm** 3-Track Trait
Gain **+1d** to take or place small items.

**Tools of the Trade** 3-Track Trait
When you use a **Prepared Action** to access tools, you take 1 less **Mire** or have **+1 Impact**.

## Socialite
#### Starting Skills
- +2 Command or Charm or Deceive
- +1 Intuit or Study
#### Traits
**Life of the Party** 3-Track
During a [[13. Long Rests|long rest]], you may give yourself or another crew member +1 long rest activity.

**Work Never Stops** 4-Track Trait
At the end of downtime, you may mark this trait to gain +2 Cash.

**On the List** 3-Track Trait
You always have access to events that require invitation.

**I know a Guy** 3-Track Trait
In a situation in which you need aid, you can mark this trait to know someone who can help where applicable. The helper has **+2d** on a skill or a **regular grade item** you can borrow. You may mark a mire to assist the helper, or mark 2 mire for a **High Grade Item.** Note this only works if the person can readily be available - for instance, you cannot use this to have someone show up inside of a rival hideout.

**Trust in me** 4-Track Trait
You get **+1d** vs. a target with whom you have an intimate relationship.

**Gossip Network** 4-Track Trait
Gain **+1 Impact** when using gossip sources throughout the city.

**Inspirational Companion** 5-Track Trait
You may mark this trait to **[[5. Mires#Aiding an Ally|aid another]]**.

## Artisan
#### Starting Skills
- +2 Manipulate or Study
- +1 Wreck, Sense, Study, Focus or Channel
#### Traits
**Well Equipped** 2-Track
You may take gear from any other profession list when choosing gear.

**Gearhead**  3-Track
When you make any gear, treat partials as full successes.

**Artificer**  2-Track
You do not take cut when making magic gear.

**Connected** 4-Track Trait
During downtime, you get **+1 impact** when you acquire an asset

**Ablative Armor** 4-Track Trait
When taking physical wounds, each track on this trait counts as two harm.

**Sound Appraisal** 4-Track Trait
You can spot weaknesses in objects, granting a **+1d** to setup actions.

## Adherent
#### Starting Skills
- +2 Focus or Will
- +1 Stalk or Endure
#### Traits
Thank the Greater Powers 
Once per job, you may take a flashback without incurring any consequences. Describe how convenient the solution is - the solution doesn't even need to be set up prior, perhaps someone dropped a key, or there is ice covered ground so your opponent slips.

## Arcane
#### Starting Skills
- +2 Focus or Will
- +1 Study or Intuit

#### Traits
**All Seeing Eye** 4-Track Trait
Push yourself and mark this trait to open your senses to the magical. This affects everyone differently, but generally you cannot be lied to from magical means. You can see traces of magic, to see the aura of others. 

**Ritualistic Casting** 2-Track Trait
When casting a ritual, you get **+1d** for the spell.

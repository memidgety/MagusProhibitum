
## Enforcer
#### Starting Skills
- +2 **Skirmish** or **Wreck**
- +1 **Command** or **Sway**
#### Traits
##### Bodyguard 
4-Track
When you protect a teammate, you may make a skill check with 1 cut to reduce the wounds by that amount. When you gather info to anticipate possible threats in the current situation, you get +1 effect.
##### Leader
3-Track
When you set up an ally, you get improved position for the action.
##### Mule
3-Track
Add +2 to your load limits; for instance Light: 5; Normal: 7; Heavy: 8;  
##### Hercules
2-Track
When you push yourself, you *display superhuman levels of strength,* and you do not take cut from Strength based actions.
##### Just a Scratch
3-Track
When you take the Heal Wounds [[12. Long Rests#Healing Wounds|long rest]] action, you heal half of your traits rounded up.
##### Warded 
1-Track
Magic affecting you takes -1 impact. When dispelling magics, you do not take cut. 

**Caution to the Wind**
4-Track Trait
Mark to increase impact when attacking at close quarters.

## Hunter
#### Starting Skills
- +2 Hunt or Skirmish
- +1 Traverse or Stalk
#### Traits
##### Focused Shot
4-Track Trait
Mark to increase impact when attacking at long range.

##### Precise Aim 
3-Track Trait
You can target attacks precisely (such as when aiming for a weak point) without cutting for difficulty.

##### High Ground 
3-Track
Increase impact on any successful action taken whilst you have the high ground.

##### Urban Jungle 
4-Track
Mark to sprint up a surface that would usually require you to climb it, even if it’s unstable.
___
## Sleuth
#### Starting Skills
- +2 Sense or Study
- +1 Stalk or Traverse
#### Traits
##### Discerning Eye 
4-Track
You can tell a fake, forgery, or counterfeit item at a glance.

##### Truthseeker 
3-Track
You can always tell when someone is lying to you.

##### Cloak & Dagger
3-Track
When you use a disguise or other form of covert misdirection, you get +1d to rolls to confuse or deflect suspicion. When you throw off your disguise, the resulting surprise gives you the initiative in the situation.

##### Mesmer
3-Track
When you [[4. Skills#SWAY|sway]] someone, you may cause them to forget that it’s happened until they next interact with you.

___
## Socialite
#### Starting Skills
- +2 Command or Sway
- +1 Sense or Study
#### Traits
##### Life of the Party
3-Track
During a [[12. Long Rests|long rest]], you may give yourself or another crew member +1 long rest activity.

## Artisan
#### Starting Skills
- +2 Create or Concoct
- +1 Wreck, Sense, Study, Focus or Channel
#### Traits
##### Well Equipped
2-Track
You may take gear from any other profession list when choosing gear.

##### Gearhead 
3-Track
When you make any gear, treat partials as full successes.

##### Artificer 
2-Track
You do not take cut when making magic gear.

## Adherent
#### Starting Skills
- +2 Focus 
- +1 Sense or Channel
#### Traits
##### Thank the Greater Powers 
Once per job, you may take a flashback without incurring any consequences. Describe how convenient the solution is - the solution doesn't even need to be set up prior, perhaps someone dropped a key, or there is ice covered ground so your opponent slips.

# Cash
An arbitrary amount of money, jewels, resources that is readily available for trade.
1 Cash: A full purse/wallet. A week of wages
2 Cash: A fine weapon, a luxurious set of clothes, a piece of art. A weekly income for a small business.
4 Cash: A bag of money. A month's wages.
6 Cash: A duffel of money, usually a burden to walk with, or an exceptionally exquisite jewel or mastercrafted work of art.
8 Cash: A good monthly take for a small business. A small safe full of cash and valuables. A very rare luxury commodity.
10 Cash: Truly a large sum of cash. The initial investment in a business, a liquidated asset such as a building or crew, or a deed to a small property.

# Fortune
The savings of a character. This represents how much money they are able to throw around on menial things, such as food, clothes, hotel stay, etc. But also represents their retirement. A higher savings allows a character to retire out of poverty and live out a life of relaxation. 

# Mana
The flow of magic is an ethereal force. But some individuals can harness this flow and store it within themselves. This is often called Mana among practitioners. Storing magic within one self is a powerful and dangerous act. This bottled magical force spends more time with the users consciousness, becoming more capable of being shaped by the wizard. Casting a spell with a store of mana does not incite the normal cut that comes along with using magic. However, if a wizard has any amount of stored mana, they take a -1d for any other action they roll - due to the amount of concentration and strain on the mind and body that the mana takes. 

Mana is difficult to obtain. Often a long stretch of time - from dozens of minutes to hours -  is needed to stockpile enough magical energies to become Mana. Wizards will often store mana in their bodies in preparation for a confrontation, though it is rumored that some wizards have learned to bottle mana into more portable forms.

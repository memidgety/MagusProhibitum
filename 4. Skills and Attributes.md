## Skill Ranks
Each of your character’s skills has a rank, starting at one and topping out at four. When used as part of an action, skills add a number of d6s to your dice pool equal to their rank plus an attribute that could be used in said action. For example, Laura uses Sway to convince a gang boss to let the crew work in his territory, but she also adds on her Vigor as she is hard headed and determined to get what she wants. 

### Crossover
There are multiple skills on this list that share some thematic space, rubbing shoulders with others in terms of what they might let you do. This is by design - it allows your skills to be more easily flavored by the attribute you choose, giving an added level of flexibility and letting you apply them to unexpected events.
In essence, try to view your skills as something you can and should bend to fit a new situation, just as you would in real life. The GM may reduce your impact or apply cut if the circumstances of using a skill are particularly tenuous, but these are limits you’ll find at your table through the course of play.

### Helping Hands
A well-put-together crew will have a variety of skills to call on, but sometimes what one character wants to do may be better-rolled by another in the group. In these cases, two crewmembers can work together on the same task, but they are both affected by any negatives that may result from rolling a conflict or disaster.

# Attributes
To determine your **attributes**, add up how many **unique skills** in each category you have. For instance, Janna has 1 Finesse, 1 Study and 1 Focus. This means she has a 1 in **Deftness**, a 2 in **Acuity**, and 0 for the other attributes.

## Attribute Boosting
Attribute Boosting, or just **Boosting** a roll, allows you to add +1d to a roll related to that attribute. However, you can only do this a number of times equal to your Attribute. This recovers when you take a long rest *(or heal tbd).*

# Toughness
Toughness represents ones physical prowess.

## Skirmish 
A skill of close-quarters combat, awareness, and controlled violence. Use Skirmish to fight tactically, trade blows, and survive chaotic engagements where positioning and timing matter as much as strength. This might take the form of…
- Exchanging blows in a tight melee
- Parrying or counterattacking an opponent
- Fighting while outnumbered or pressed
- Holding your own in a brawl or due
## Endure
A skill of defense, hard-headedness, and immobility. Use Endure to weather the ravages of weather, exhaustion, and those that want to see you fall. This might take the form of...
- Clinging to life in the toughest situations
- Shrugging off attempts to manipulate or confuse you
- Blocking or parrying an incoming attack
- Shouldering your way through a crowd
- Taking the brunt of The Flow to channel a spell
## Break 
A skill of fragments, shockwaves, and destruction. Use Break to smash through stone, metal, and machinery, working against the efforts of civilization. This might take the form of...
- Reducing a building or obstacle to rubble
- Charting a course through ruins and wreckage
- Relying on hammers and fists in a skirmish
- Assessing a structure's stability and weak points
- Setting and disarming explosives

# Deftness 

## Finesse
A skill of precision, flourishes, and showmanship. Use Finesse for making controlled movements, careful consideration, and performances. This might take the form of...
- Thrusting a rapier
- Dancing with a partner
- Talking someone down from a confrontation
- Create a delicate spell
## Manipulate 
A skill of subtle movement. Use Manipulate to unlock doors, convince someone you are correct, or move someone into (or out of) danger. Examples of Manipulate take the form of...
- Creating a watch
- Fixing a firearm
- Lie to the bouncer
- Grapple someone's weapon from them
## Stalk
A skill of shadows, stealth, and observation. Use stalk to remain unseen, follow someone, or keep a lookout. Example of Stalk take the form of...
- Remaining in the shadows as a room fills with light
- To listen in on a conversation unnoticed
- Watch for a patrol of guards
- Figure out if someone was here

# Instinct 
Innate knowledge, reaction speed, intuition and awareness
## Hunt
A skill of pursuit, patience, and lethal intent. Use Hunt to track targets, line up decisive strikes, and engage at range or from advantage. This might take the form of…
- Following tracks or signs to their source
- Lining up a careful rifle or bow shot
- Anticipating an enemy’s movement
- Laying in wait for the right moment
## React
A skill of speed, reflexes, and split-second decisions. Use React to lever speed, find a target that was just lost, dodge, and get yourself out of a situation. This might take the form of...
- Blocking an attack
- Adjust your aim to a moving target
- Find where someone went as you're chasing them
- Blindly commit an action with speed
- Throw up a magical ward
## Intuit 
A skill of awareness, instinct, and perception. Use Sense to notice danger, read environments, and catch what others miss. This might take the form of…
- Spotting an ambush or hidden threat
- Noticing subtle changes in mood or space
- Hearing something out of place
- Reacting quickly to sudden danger
- Attuning to essence to feel for magics

# Presence 
Charisma, persuasion, guile, and confidence.
## Command
A skill of authority, leadership, and force of personality. Use Command to direct others, establish authority, and rally those around you. Command might take the form of...
- Rally allies under fire
- Issue orders to subordinates
- Intimidate someone into backing down
- Assert authority over a crowd
## Charm
A skill of charisma, empathy, rapport, and social manipulation. Use Charm to ingratiate yourself, negotiate, seduce, and build relationships. Charm might take the form of...
- Convince someone to do you a favor
- Negotiate a better deal
- Make a favorable first impression
- Seduce or flatter someone
- Put an uneasy stranger at ease
## Will
A skill of confidence, conviction, and self-assurance. Use Will to stand your ground, resist pressure, and project your **will** onto the world. Will might take the form of...
- Resist interrogation or intimidation
- Stand firm against coercion
- Defy an authority or overwhelming odds
- Maintain composure in the face of horror
- Shape a spell to your bidding

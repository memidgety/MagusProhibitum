In Magus Prohibitum, the party enacts jobs to further their standing in life. The company can be something that lasts beyond a character's life - a legacy. 

A Job is an operation with a single goal in mind, and be started by any character or given by a client. Jobs should be discussed by the party then decided on, as this is the main method of play in Magus Prohibitum.

Example jobs:
- Find a murderer (or commit one)
- Retrieve files from a secured location
- Expand the Org by getting a warehouse
- Stopping a rival organization selling goods on your territory
- Searching for a magical artifact

A job can be as long or short as the job needs - a few hours to weeks if necessary. 

The Job has a few interesting mechanics of note: Planning, The Drop ,and Prepared Actions.

# Planning
Players can spend any saved downtime actions

# The Drop
To speed up travel from point A to B, the Drop puts the characters directly in the job at their first real obstacle.

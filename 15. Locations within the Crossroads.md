# The Circle
The main commercial hub and center of activity in the City of Crossroads

# Uptown
Has close proximity to The Circle. Vibrant retail, restaurants and nightlife; easy public access; high-rise living and many museums and parks are located here.
# Old Town

# Underloft
Comprising of nearly 60 percent of The City of Crossroad's land area, the Underloft is by far the largest part of the city by land area. While this part of the city has a reputation for crime, Underloft is a diverse area that is home to communities ranging from poor to affluent. Despite what you may have heard, the Underloft has a lot of attractions and amenities that make it a great place to live or visit.

# Castle Walk
The industrial center of The Crossroads. Located along the river and closest to the Citadel, many businesses conduct trade at the waters edge. The proximity to the Citadel means that one can find almost anything they would need within reach. 

# Easthill
The wealthiest part of town. Many longer lived races live here being supported by generational wealth. Several of the wealthiest shops and restaurants are found here. 

# Northshore
A hub for shops, artisans, commerce, and socializing.

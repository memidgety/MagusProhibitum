These most notable factions in the City of Crossroads are briefly described below with specifics found later in the chapter.

# Faction Tiers
A Factions **Tier** represents the size of that faction.

# Factions at a glance
## Institutions
___
CCPD **T4** - The City of Crossroads Police Department. They are either the solvers or source of many problems in the city.

Government Sanctioned Wizards **T3** - Known as Grey Hats for their large grey brimmed hats, these Sanctioned Wizards do research and steer people towards technology rather than magic.

Black Cloaks T3 - Opposite to the Grey Hats, the Black Coats are warlock hunters, searching out unsanctioned wizards and arresting those who use magic casually.

The Evening Ledger T3 - The City's most influential paper, and the army of reporters they employ.

Observational Security Recon and Analysis (ObScuRA) T5 -  The States Arcana's team of spies and agents in the City of Crossroads. Not much is known about them, except that they do not follow traditional ethics and have many wizards in there employ.

White Chapel T3 - A board of the most wealthiest noble elites, aimed at steering the City of Crossroads to the future they see fit.

Odawatomic University T4 - The City of Crossroads most prestigious school. Researchers and students from all over take up residence here to chart the unknown. 

Green Wardens T3 - Doctors for good that help any in need.\

Magnified Optics T2 - A group of detectives working to bring to light the unsolvable crimes of the city

Ulysses Verne - T1 A lone detective punching way above his weight

## Underworld
---
Adrian Moscone - Crime Lord of the City of Crossroads

Drug Lords - 

The Misters - A group of smugglers that use the early mist of the river to conceal their movements

Executers - Dirty cops pushed over the edge, now hunting criminals and warlocks and committing horrid execution scenes with those they deem a stain on society. 

Scarred Survivors - Old homeless survivors of the Magus War. They help those in need, but vehemently dislike wizards - sanctioned or not.

Red Street - A row of vice dens run by those with more primal urges, one can find any way to loose themselves on this street.

Shilly's - A Mistress adjacent to Red Street, has thralls.

Pixie Dusts - Gang of mostly Fey Bruisers. They user Knuckle Dusters.

The Blackened Hand - A group of anarchists fighting for the reversal of Magus Prohibitum

18th street - Poverty stricken soldiers who have not gotten aid from the government that used them



## Mystics
---
Old Town Coven - Witches that practice magic illegally to better the lives of those in poverty.

The Collective - Artists who push the limits of art - regardless of ethics and legality.

Saint Mary's Church - Devout followers of religion who aim to help those in need.

The Observatory - The bar owned by Tyuritel "Ty" Lake, is not just a fine bar with a sight of the sky, but Sanctioned Ground where parties of differing sides may come to talk.

These most notable factions in the City of Crossroads are briefly described below with specifics found later in the chapter.

# Factions at a glance
## Institutions
___
CCPD

Government Sanctioned Wizards

Warlock hunters

The Observatory - The bar owned by Tyrrell "Ty" Lake, is not just a fine bar with a sight of the sky, but Sanctioned Ground where parties of differing sides may come to talk.

Observational Security Recon and Analysis (ObScuRA) -  The USAs team of spys and agents in the City of Crossroads. Not much is known about them, except that they do not follow traditional ethics and have many wizards in there employ.

White Chapel - A board of the most wealthiest noble elites, aimed at steering the City of Crossroads to the future they see fit.

## Underworld
---
Adrian Moscone - Crime Lord of the City of Crossroads

Drug Lords - 

Executers - Dirty cops pushed over the edge, now hunting criminals and warlocks and committing horrid execution scenes with those they deem a stain on society. 

Scarred Survivors - Old homeless survivors of the Magus War. They help those in need, but vehemently dislike wizards - sanctioned or not.

Red Street - A row of vice dens run by those with more primal urges, one can find any way to loose themselves on this street.
## Mystics
---
Old Town Coven - Witches that practice magic illegally to better the lives of those in poverty.

The Collective - Artists who push the limits of art - regardless of ethics and legality.

Saint Mary's Church - Devout followers of religion who aim to help those in need.

## Industrial
---
The Dock Workers

Factory Union - The factory workers union, fighting for fair working conditions in all workplaces

Skywalkers - Iron workers for the upward expansion of skyscrapers taking root in the Crossroads.

Coachers - The public coach operators. The go to place for gossip.

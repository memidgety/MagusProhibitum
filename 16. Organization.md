The Organization, or Org for short, is what all the characters are working for. Whether made by one of the characters, or a legacy passed down to them, the Org is there for something greater. Together you all buy into the Org and make it grow and expand, making names for yourselves.

The Org is the driving motive of the party. If someone leaves, retires, or passes, another character can join the Organization to fill their place. This also leaves room for multiple characters per player, or more players that rotate out as the story unfolds.

In this chapter, different organization types will be discussed.

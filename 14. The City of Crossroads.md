The City of Crossroads started as just a small town of hundreds, but as people fled west with Arcane States new purchase of land, the City grew as a hub, becoming the second largest city in the country. It has withstood wars, plagues, fires, and disasters, always rebuilding.

The city is packed with construction. After the fires of 1871 fueled by the wood of early development, people turned to steel. The birth of the skyscraper turned sparse low streets, to towering dense urban sprawls. Trains and boats arrive every hour, nonstop to fuel the unending growth of the city. People from all races and backgrounds cross paths every second in search of a brighter future. 

The City of Crossroads is important to the United States of Arcana as it is a City located on the Great Lakes, but closest to the Great River, joining the Atlantic to the great westward expansion. From here, supplies are sent from the industrious east, to the growing settlers and explorers west.  Because of the importance of commerce, many barons of industry have set up in The City of Crossroads to profit from the enterprise of the lower class.
